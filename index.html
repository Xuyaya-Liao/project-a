Electronic Colony

Scientific Name:

ELectronic Colony

Discovered by / at:

Lyris Liao, 2026.10.15

Family:

Digital Fungi

Habitat:

E-Colony lives in a digital petri dish, where it grows, spreads, and interacts with other colonies.

Appearance:

E-Colony is made of colorful circles and rings in different sizes. Each colony has a unique pattern and color combination.

Key Characteristics:

  • Grows and spreads after the first click.
  • Smaller colonies gradually take on the colors of larger colonies when they touch.
  • Shakes and pushes the cursor away when clicked.
  • Produces branching digital mycelium under a microscope.

What we know so far about E-Colony? E-Colony is a digital organism inspired by fungi. It begins growing after the user introduces the first seed into the petri dish. Each colony has its own size, color, and growth speed, creating a unique pattern every time. When colonies meet, their colors slowly change, showing how they interact with each other. E-Colony can also react to human interaction, almost like a living creature.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Click anywhere to start growing E-Colony. Click on a colony to see it shake and push your cursor away. You can also drag to create a microscope area and observe the growth of digital mycelium.
